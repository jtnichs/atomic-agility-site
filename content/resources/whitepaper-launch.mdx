Every framework for scaling agility has gotten very good at aligning systems—
portfolios to programs, programs to teams, strategy to delivery. But there's a
layer the models keep skipping: the individual. Systems scale, but people
perform. When the work gets disrupted faster than the org chart can adapt, it's
people who absorb the shock, and right now most of them are doing it without a
clear line between their own growth and the enterprise's direction.

The Individual Alignment Layer is Atomic Agility's answer to that gap, and it
arrives at a moment when the gap is widening fast. AI is rewriting what roles
mean, which skills matter, and how quickly any of it changes. This isn't a
trend piece written to ride the wave—it's the natural extension of a framework
built for exactly this kind of disruption. As a SAFe coach who has spent years
helping federal agencies and enterprise teams stay adaptive through real change,
John Nichols wrote this to give leaders a practical way to connect individual
performance and growth to the delivery outcomes the business is already chasing.

The full whitepaper lays out the model, the practices behind it, and how to put
it to work inside an existing SAFe implementation. Download the complete PDF
below to read it in full.
